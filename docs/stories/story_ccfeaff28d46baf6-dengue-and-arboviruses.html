Historical Epidemiology Context

Urban Spread

Urban spread changes the outbreak problem: delays, care seeking, contact tracing, hospital exposure, rumor control, and institutional coordination all become harder at once.

Suspected Vs Confirmed Counts

Early outbreak counts reflect case definitions, testing access, laboratory turnaround, and retrospective case finding. Suspected cases can rise before confirmation catches up, and cleaned totals can change after field investigation.

From vaccine availability to effective deployment: Closing the arboviral vaccine access gap.

Using country-level classifications of regulatory approval and national immunization program inclusion, we identify substantial gaps between vaccine availability and areas at greatest epidemiological risk. The central challenge extends beyond vaccine feasibility to a multilayered vaccine-access gap spanning initial availability, sustained deployment and alignment with transmission risk. Mosquito-borne viral diseases are expanding globally, yet programmatic access to dengue and chikungunya vaccines remains limited or uneven in many high-risk settings.
