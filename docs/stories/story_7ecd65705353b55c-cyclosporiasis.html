Trump's pick for FDA faces scrutiny after cyclospora outbreak

Location: United States

President Donald Trump's new choice to lead the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) faces a tough road ahead, after several foodborne illness outbreaks this summer left many Americans sick and frustrated with their government. While such outbreaks occur each year, when warmer conditions fuel growth of bacteria, experts say the cyclospora infections have been unprecedented and health officials have seemed slower to identify contamination issues.
